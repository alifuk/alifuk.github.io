2. What the App Does

LuneFit lets you:

  • Browse wedding dresses
  • Create AI-generated virtual tryout previews
  • Save generated images
  • Contact Lune Boutique through phone, Instagram, WhatsApp, or the Lune tab in the app

Virtual tryout images are AI-generated previews for inspiration only. They are not a real fitting and may be inaccurate.

3. Personal Data We Process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Photos you select from your device for virtual tryouts
  • Dress images used to create virtual tryout previews
  • AI-generated tryout result images
  • App install ID used for tryout limits and activation codes
  • Usage information related to the number of virtual tryouts used and unlocked
  • Messages or contact information you choose to send us through WhatsApp, phone, Instagram, or other contact methods
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app and external image generation service

The app does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. Please do not upload photos or content that you do not want processed for virtual tryout generation.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the virtual tryout feature
  • To generate and display AI-created tryout previews
  • To save your selected photo and generated tryout images locally in the app
  • To manage free tryout limits and activation codes
  • To let you contact Lune Boutique about dresses, appointments, or additional tryouts
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app
  • To comply with legal obligations and App Store requirements

5. AI Image Processing

When you use the virtual tryout feature, your selected photo and the relevant dress image may be sent to an external image generation provider to create the virtual tryout result.

This processing is necessary to provide the virtual tryout feature. The generated output may not accurately represent real fit, size, material, color, texture, availability, or final appearance of the dress.

We do not use virtual tryout images for advertising tracking. We do not sell your photos.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a service you request: to generate virtual tryout previews and provide app functionality
  • Consent: when you choose to upload a photo and agree to the app terms before using virtual tryouts
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app
  • Legal obligations: when we need to comply with applicable laws

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, but this may prevent the app from providing virtual tryout features.

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Data stored locally in the app may remain on your device until you delete it, delete generated tryouts, remove your image, or uninstall the app.

Messages sent through WhatsApp, Instagram, phone, or other external services are handled according to those services’ own privacy policies.
